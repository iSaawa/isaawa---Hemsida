IT & Game Projects

The IT and game development side of iSaawa is created by Niclas and Casper - a father and son who share a strong interest in technology, coding and games. By bringing together two generations of ideas, experience and perspectives, we develop digital projects and tools with a focus on players and their experiences.

Our first project is called Weaponizer and is currently under development. The project will be tested by an international group of players before we are ready to share more and present it publicly.

Weaponizer is the beginning of our shared journey. For us, the work is not only about technology, but also about the time we create together as father and son. In the future, we want to continue developing smart, useful and entertaining solutions within IT, coding and games.

Weaponizer

Weaponizer is our first game-enhancement project – a tool that allows players to compare weapons, attacks and other options before making costly in-game purchases. The service is being prepared for international testing and is planned to become a subscription service in the future.
